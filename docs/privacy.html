Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Archiva ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered document management tool. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

  • Account info: Email address and name when you sign up.
  • Documents: Files you upload to your workspace.
  • Usage data: Basic interaction data to improve the service.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the document management and AI assistant features.
  • To authenticate your account.
  • To send transactional emails (verification, password reset).

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Authentication and database hosting.
  • Google OAuth: Optional sign-in method. We only receive your email and name.

Data Storage

Your documents and data are stored securely. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

Contact

For questions about this policy, reach out via the app or through our GitHub repository.
